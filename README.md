## 🇨🇳 中文

15 秒以内、音频或参考视频驱动的完整二次元 PV / MV / 一图流 / 文字动画 PV / 动态设计 MG 制作技能。启动先用卡片让用户选开场类型并确认能提供的素材（纯音频＋想法、纯音频＋视频参考、立绘＋分镜图参考、参考动画）。随后分析音频或参考视频音轨，判断歌词、鼓点、弦乐、节奏点与情绪段落；有歌词就索要准确歌词并匹配节奏，纯音乐则自动处理鼓点与弦乐。参考图/参考视频会自动解析美术风格并反解 prompt，立绘作为分镜主角元素，最终输出 9/16 宫格分镜、动态设计长提示词、H3 片段与完整卡点成片。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 纯音频、参考视频、立绘或分镜图参考 → 15 秒以内完整 PV、MV、一图流、文字动画 PV 或动态设计 MG
- 开场卡片先选类型（MV／一图流与文字动画 PV／动态设计 MG），再确认素材角色，未选完不进入高成本生成
- 音频分析判断歌词、鼓点、弦乐、节奏点与情绪段落；有歌词则逐句匹配节奏
- 参考图/参考视频自动反解美术风格与 prompt，立绘作为分镜主角元素，分镜图作 MG/PV 单帧简略演示
- 输出 9/16 宫格分镜、动态设计长提示词、H3 片段与完整卡点成片，反复迭代到满意
- 视频模型锁死 H3 最高精度；不可用时必须询问用户等待或临时更换，不静默替换

## 🇬🇧 English

A complete anime PV / MV / one-image / text-animation PV / motion-design MG skill driven by audio or reference video up to 15 seconds. It opens with type-selection cards and confirms which assets you can provide — audio plus an idea, audio plus a video reference, a character illustration plus storyboard references, or reference animation. It then analyses the audio or the reference video's track to identify lyrics, drums, strings, beat points and emotional sections; with lyrics it asks for the exact words and matches them to the beat, and for instrumental tracks it handles drums and strings automatically. Reference images and videos are parsed for art style and reverse-engineered into prompts, illustrations become the storyboard's lead element, and the output is 9/16-grid panels, long motion-design prompts, H3 clips and a fully beat-synced cut.

### ✨ What it does

- Audio only, reference video, an illustration or storyboard references → a complete PV, MV, one-image, text-animation PV or motion-design MG under 15 seconds
- Opening cards pick the type (MV / one-image and text-animation PV / motion-design MG), then confirm asset roles; nothing costly starts before that
- Audio analysis identifies lyrics, drums, strings, beat points and emotional sections; lyrics are matched line by line to the beat
- Reference images and videos are reverse-engineered into art-style prompts; illustrations lead the storyboard and storyboard references act as single-frame MG/PV demos
- Outputs 9/16-grid panels, long motion-design prompts, H3 clips and a fully beat-synced cut, iterated until it holds up
- The video model is locked to top-precision H3; if unavailable it must ask whether to wait or switch, never substitute silently
